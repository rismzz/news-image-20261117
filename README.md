"The World — In One Image", round 2 — a poster that sums up the world
news of Tuesday 17 November 2026 in a single 1024x1024 image: Ukraine's
destruction of the Kerch Bridge road arch leads, above colour-coded
panels on the U.S. strike on Iran's Qeshm Island, the first-ever
Xi–Trump–Putin meeting in Shenzhen, the United States losing its
measles-free status after 26 years, COP31's fossil-fuel fight, and AI
stocks dragging Wall Street down — plus an "Also Today" strip. The
poster is rendered with PIL from `render_poster.py`; `main.py` copies
the finished `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte, on
every run. No network, no randomness.

## Run it

```sh
python3 render_poster.py   # re-render entry.png (optional)
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
